---
title: Access, SSO and billing
description: What the agent can reach, how SSO verification works, and how billing affects it.
---

The agent has exactly your access: the same organizations, projects, environments and [roles](/team/understanding-roles)
as you have in the console. If you can't see or change something in the console, the agent can't either.

## Roles

- Organization Owners and Admins can reach every project, unless they've been
  [restricted](/team/owner-admin-restrictions) in a project or environment.
- Members only see projects they've been added to, and [protected environments](/environment/protected-environments)
  they're part of.
- What the agent can change depends on your project role. See the [minimum role for each tool](/mcp/tools).

## Single sign-on (SSO)

If an organization uses [SSO](/team/single-sign-on), each connected client has to verify through your identity provider
before it can use that organization.

- **The agent gives you a link.** When you ask about an SSO organization, the agent replies with a verification link.
  Open it in the browser where you're signed in to LocalOps and complete the SSO sign-in.
- **The link is valid for 15 minutes.** If it expires, ask the agent for a new one.
- **Verification lasts until your SSO sign-in expires**, up to 24 hours. That's counted from when you signed in through
  SSO, not from when you opened the link.
- **You may not see your IdP.** If you're already signed in to your identity provider, verification can finish straight
  away without a prompt.
- **Each client verifies on its own.** Verifying in Claude doesn't verify Cursor. Reconnecting a client means verifying
  again.

<Warning>
  Removing someone from the LocalOps app in your identity provider stops new verifications, but doesn't end one that's
  already active. To remove access straight away, disconnect their client or [remove them from the
  organization](/team/remove-user).
</Warning>

## Billing

The agent follows the same billing rules as the console.

| Billing state      | Owners and Admins                     | Members                               |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Needs subscription | Can't create environments or services | Can't create environments or services |
| Cancelled          | Read only                             | Read only                             |
| Payment overdue    | Read only                             | No access to the organization         |

Plan limits apply too, such as the number of environments and services on your plan. When the agent is refused, it tells
you why and links you to the console.
