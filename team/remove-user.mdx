---
title: Remove a user
description: Remove someone from your LocalOps organization
---

Owners and Admins can remove people from the organization:

1. In the sidebar, under **Organization**, go to **Settings** → **Members**.
2. Find the person and click **⋯** → **Actions** on their row.
3. Choose **Remove member**.
4. Confirm with **Continue**.

They lose access immediately - to the organization, every project and every protected environment.

<Note>
  Only an Owner can remove another Owner, and an Owner can't remove themselves. The organization always keeps at least
  one Owner.
</Note>

To take someone out of a single project without removing them from the organization,
[remove them from the project](/team/project-members#remove-someone-from-the-project) instead.
