---
title: Owner and Admin restrictions
description: Lower an Owner's or Admin's access in a single project or environment, and lift it again
---

Owners and Admins have full access to every project. Sometimes that is more than they need. An Admin who looks after
billing shouldn't be one click away from deleting production, and you may want to keep yourself from changing a project
by accident.

A **restriction** lowers an Owner's or Admin's access in one project, or in one
[protected environment](/environment/protected-environments), to Member, Maintainer or Read-only. Everywhere else they
keep full access, and they stay an Owner or Admin of the organization.

While restricted, they can do exactly what that [project role](/team/understanding-roles#permissions-in-detail) can do.

## Rules

| Who                                                          | Can restrict                                     | Can lift             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| An Owner or Admin, for **themselves**                        | Always, to any role - higher or lower than today | Always               |
| Someone with the Member role in the project (or environment) | Other Owners and Admins                          | -                    |
| An Owner or Admin with **full access** in the project        | Other Owners and Admins                          | Anyone's restriction |

When restricting **someone else**:

- The first time, any role can be picked.
- If they are already restricted, the role can only be **lowered**. To raise it, an Owner or Admin with full access has
  to lift the restriction - after which it can be set again.

<Note>
  A restriction can't be used to lock everyone out. The restricted person can always lift their own restriction, and any
  Owner or Admin with full access in the project can lift it for them.
</Note>

Owners and Admins can't be removed from a project - restricting them is how you limit them there.

## Restrict yourself in a project

1. Open the project and go to **Settings** → **Members**.
2. Click **⋯** on your own row and choose **Change role**.
3. In **Restrict your access**, pick Member, Maintainer or Read-only and click **Update role**.

To change it later, do the same - the dialog is called **Change your restriction**, and you can pick any role, higher or
lower.

## Restrict another Owner or Admin in a project

You need the Member role in the project, or full access.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Members**.
2. Click **⋯** on their row and choose **Change role**.
3. In **Restrict access**, pick their role and click **Update role**.

If they're already restricted, only their current role and lower roles are offered.

## Lift a restriction

Click **⋯** on the restricted person's row and choose **Lift restriction**. It takes effect immediately, with no
confirmation, and they have full access to the project again.

**Lift restriction** works on your own row, and an Owner or Admin with full access can use it on anyone's row.

Lifting a project restriction also removes the person's roles in that project's protected environments, so they get full
access there too.

## Restrictions in a protected environment

The same rules apply inside a [protected environment](/environment/protected-environments), from the environment's
**Members** tab:

| Action                                 | Menu item                   |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Restrict yourself                      | **Restrict your access**    |
| Restrict another Owner or Admin        | **Restrict access**         |
| Change your own restriction (any role) | **Change your restriction** |
| Lower someone else's restriction       | **Lower restriction**       |
| Lift a restriction                     | **Lift restriction**        |

Restricting someone in a protected environment only changes their access in that environment. Unprotecting the
environment removes every restriction in it.

## How restrictions show up

In **Settings** → **Members**, a restricted Owner or Admin shows their restricted role with an **Org owner** or **Org
admin** badge and a **Restricted** badge. An unrestricted one shows **Full access**.

## Restrictions and organization role changes

- **Member → Admin or Owner:** their project roles and environment roles are cleared, so they start with full access
  everywhere.
- **Admin ↔ Owner:** their project restrictions are cleared. Restrictions in protected environments stay until lifted.
- **Owner or Admin → Member:** they become a regular Member. You pick their project role in each project they're in, and
  their environment roles are cleared - add them to protected environments again if they still need them. See
  [change someone's organization role](/team/invite-users#change-someones-organization-role).
