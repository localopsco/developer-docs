---
title: Invite team members
---

Follow these steps to invite your team to LocalOps.

#### Steps to Invite Your Team:

1. Sign in to LocalOps.
2. Click on your profile located in the bottom left corner.
3. Choose **Account Settings** from the menu.
4. Enter your team member's email address.
5. Pick their [organization role](/team/understanding-roles#organization-roles) - Owner, Admin or Member.
6. Click on **Invite member**.
7. Your team member will receive an invite link via email.
8. Once they verify the link, they'll gain access to your account.

Only Owners and Admins can invite new users to the organization and cancel pending invites.

#### Add someone to a project

Organization Members only see the projects they've been added to. To give someone access to a project:

1. Open the project and go to **Settings → Members**.
2. Add an existing organization member.
3. Pick their [project role](/team/understanding-roles#project-roles) - Member, Maintainer or Read-only.

Owners and Admins already have access to every project. You can
[restrict them](/team/understanding-roles#owners-and-admins-in-a-project) in a project instead.
