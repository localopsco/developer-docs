---
title: Protected environments
description: Limit an environment, such as production, to the people you choose, with a role of its own
---

By default an environment belongs to the whole project. Everyone in the project can see it, with their
[project role](/team/understanding-roles#project-roles).

**Protecting** an environment changes that. Only these people can see a protected environment:

- Owners and Admins with full access to the project
- The people you add to the environment

Everyone else - including people with the Member role in the project - loses access to it until they're added. Use it
for environments such as production, where only a few people should deploy or read secrets.

## What changes for people who aren't added

A protected environment is hidden from them completely:

- It isn't listed under the project's environments.
- Its deployments and operations aren't listed either.
- Opening its link shows **Environment not found**.
- They can't pick it as a source for **Import from environment**.
- The CLI can't reach it.

Owners and Admins who are [restricted](/team/owner-admin-restrictions) in the project are treated like everyone else
here: they need to be added.

## Environment roles

Everyone you add gets an **environment role** - Read-only, Maintainer or Member. Inside that environment it replaces
their project role, and it can be higher or lower than it. For example, a project Maintainer can be given Read-only on
production, and a project Read-only can be given Maintainer on a protected staging environment.

What each role can do there is the same as in the [roles tables](/team/understanding-roles#permissions-in-detail):
deploying needs Maintainer, secret values need Member, and so on. The [CLI](/cli/usage#who-can-run-which-command) uses
the environment role too.

Only people who are already in the project can be added. To give someone access to a protected environment,
[add them to the project](/team/project-members) first.

## Protect an environment

You need the Member role in the project, or full access.

1. Open the environment.
2. Click **Protect** in the header.
3. Read the confirmation and click **Protect**.

If you're not an Owner or Admin with full access, you're added to the environment with your current project role, so you
keep access.

Protected environments show a **Protected** badge, in the environment header and next to its name in the environments
list.

<Warning>
  Everyone without full access loses the environment as soon as it's protected - their open pages stop working and the
  CLI can no longer reach it. Add the people who need it right after protecting.
</Warning>

## Add people and set their role

Open the environment's **Members** tab. It only appears on protected environments.

The list shows everyone who could have access - the project's members and the organization's Owners and Admins:

| Column               | What it shows                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **User**             | Name and email, with an **Org owner** or **Org admin** badge for Owners and Admins                                                                       |
| **Project role**     | Their role in the project                                                                                                                                |
| **Environment role** | The role set for them in this environment, or a dash if they don't have one                                                                              |
| **Access here**      | What they can do here, with where it comes from underneath: **Full access (owner/admin)**, **Set for this environment**, **Restricted** or **No access** |

To add someone, click **⋯** on their row, choose **Add to environment**, pick a role and click **Add**.

To change a role, choose **Change environment role**, pick the new role and click **Save**.

To take someone out, choose **Remove from environment**. They can no longer see the environment.

You need the Member role in the environment, or full access, to add, change or remove people.

## Owners and Admins in a protected environment

Owners and Admins with full access don't need adding. Setting an environment role on one of them is a **restriction** -
it lowers their access in this environment only, following the [restriction rules](/team/owner-admin-restrictions):

- Anyone can restrict themselves, to any role, and lift their own restriction.
- Restricting another Owner or Admin needs the Member role in the environment, or full access. If they're already
  restricted, their role can only be lowered.
- Only the person themselves or an Owner or Admin with full access can lift a restriction, with **Lift restriction**.

## Unprotect an environment

You need the Member role in the environment, or full access.

1. Open the environment.
2. Click **Unprotect** in the header.
3. Confirm with **Unprotect**.

<Warning>
  Unprotecting deletes every environment role in it, including Owner and Admin restrictions. Everyone goes back to their
  project role. If you protect the environment again later, you have to add people again.
</Warning>

## Who can do what

| Action                                          | Needs                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| See a protected environment                     | Full access, or an environment role            |
| Protect an environment                          | Member role in the project, or full access     |
| Unprotect an environment                        | Member role in the environment, or full access |
| Add, change or remove people                    | Member role in the environment, or full access |
| Restrict yourself, or lift your own restriction | Any Owner or Admin                             |
| Restrict another Owner or Admin                 | Member role in the environment, or full access |
| Lift another Owner's or Admin's restriction     | Full access                                    |

**Full access** means an Owner or Admin who isn't restricted in the project or the environment.
